## Original work

All scene graphics, the pelican/bicycle rig, interface, text layout and favicon
are drawn by this project's original code. The original score, instrument
models, synthetic voice, tape effects and sound effects are generated by
`audio/` using additive synthesis, FM and seeded noise. No external image,
video, voice, music, sound-effect assets or third-party audio libraries have
been added. Historical artwork references guide the twenty completed scenes.

The project has not yet chosen a public license for its own code or finished
film. Third-party components remain subject to the licenses above.
